Farm Education

Practical, environmentally-friendly guidance for common Kenyan farm challenges.

Fall Armyworm (Maize) +

Symptoms: Ragged holes on leaves, sawdust-like frass in the funnel, yellowing plants.

Eco-friendly control: Hand-pick larvae early. Apply neem-based spray. Encourage natural predators like earwigs and birds. Avoid broad-spectrum pesticides.

Tomato Blight +

Symptoms: Brown spots on leaves, white mould under leaves, rotting fruit.

Eco-friendly control: Remove affected leaves. Improve airflow by spacing plants. Use copper-based organic spray. Rotate crops.

East Coast Fever (Cattle) +

Symptoms: High fever, swollen lymph nodes, loss of appetite, difficulty breathing.

Eco-friendly control: Use tick-resistant breeds. Regular acaricide dipping. Pasture rotation. Consult a local vet.
